## From an idea to evidence

Doctrine gives AI coding agents reliable ways to handle common engineering work. This wiki helps you understand each approach, choose the right skill, guide the work, and check the result yourself.

## What Doctrine is not

Doctrine does not replace your AI model, coding client, tests, permissions, or judgment. A skill gives an agent a way to approach the work. It cannot grant access or prove the result is correct.
