## From an idea to evidence

Doctrine gives AI coding agents reusable engineering disciplines. This wiki helps you understand what those disciplines do, choose the right skill, guide the work, and verify the result yourself.

## What Doctrine is not

Doctrine does not replace your AI model, coding client, tests, permissions, or judgment. A skill tells an agent how to approach work. It does not grant access or prove that the result is correct.
